Pricing formula

Embroidery pricing

Machine time = stitches ÷ speed + colour changes × minutes each.
Price per piece = handling + machine time × hourly rate, never less than the minimum, rounded up.
Order total = job setup + pieces × price per piece.

The numbers shown are starting values, not your rates. Set your own, then check them with the test below.
